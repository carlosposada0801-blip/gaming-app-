Whiteout Ridge

Three thousand vertical meters stand between Base Camp and the summit. Climb before the cold, the dark and the thin air stop you.

  • Every move costs stamina. Ice costs more, and you'll slip if you run dry on it. Rest on snowy ledges to recover.
  • Warmth drains with height, storms and nightfall. Camps every 500 m warm and heal you.
  • Above 7,500 m you are in the death zone. Grab oxygen canisters.
  • A red marker means rockfall in that column. Move aside.
  • After dusk, bivouac at a camp to wait for dawn.
